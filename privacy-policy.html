Official Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At QR HUB (developed by Krishna Tech Hub), your privacy is our top priority. We believe in total data sovereignty — your data belongs to you and remains on your device.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

QR HUB does not collect, sell, or store any personal identifiers, contact details, scanned content, or browsing history on external servers. All operations (scanning, generating, history logs) execute locally offline on your device.

2. App Permissions & Usage Rationale

  • Camera Access (CAMERA): Required exclusively for real-time computer vision barcode and QR scanning. Video frames are analyzed in memory and immediately discarded.
  • Photos / Media Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Used only when you intentionally pick an image from your gallery to scan a QR code.
  • Wi-Fi State Access (CHANGE_WIFI_STATE): Used only to automatically connect your device to a network when you scan a valid Wi-Fi QR code.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Required for displaying advertisement banners and interstitials via certified ad networks.

3. Third-Party Advertising Partners

To keep QR HUB 100% free, we integrate industry-standard compliant advertising networks that may collect anonymized advertising IDs in accordance with their privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy & Terms
  • Start.io (StartApp): Start.io Privacy Policy

4. Security & Offline Storage

Your scan and generate history is stored locally in an encrypted Room SQLite database on your device. You can clear your complete history at any time with a single tap.
