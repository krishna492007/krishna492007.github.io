Privacy Policy

01

Introduction & Core Philosophy

Welcome to QR HUB ("we", "our", or "us"), crafted and published by Krishna Tech Hub. We believe your privacy is fundamental. QR HUB is built from the ground up to be an ultra-fast, offline-first barcode & QR studio that processes your data directly on your device without transmitting private information to central servers.

02

Device Permissions We Access

QR HUB strictly requests permissions that are required for scanning and export functions:

⚡ Camera Permission (CAMERA)

Used solely in real-time by Google ML Kit computer vision to scan QR codes and product barcodes. No video frames, photographs, or camera feeds are ever recorded, saved, or uploaded to any remote server.

🖼️ Storage / Photos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES)

Used strictly on-demand when you choose to scan a barcode from your gallery or save generated QR codes and printable PDF sticker sheets to your device.

03

Local Data Storage & Security

All scanned barcodes, generated codes, and user preferences are stored 100% locally in your device's private SQLite Room Database. You maintain full ownership and control of your records at all times.

  • No User Account Required: You can use all core features without signing up, entering email addresses, or creating credentials.
  • 1-Tap Complete History Purge: You can delete individual scan history items or clear your entire database instantly from within the app settings.
04

Third-Party SDKs & Advertising

To support ongoing maintenance and keep QR HUB 100% free for users worldwide, the application integrates industry-standard advertising SDKs (such as Start.io / Google AdMob):

These third-party ad networks may collect anonymized technical diagnostic identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID, IP address, and network carrier) solely for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and frequency capping in strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.
