Official Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At PDF HUB (developed by Krishna Tech Hub), your privacy is our top priority. We believe in total data sovereignty — your data belongs to you and never leaves your device.

1. Zero Data Collection

PDF HUB does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, file contents, metadata, usage analytics, or device identifiers. All PDF and image processing operations execute entirely offline on your device. No servers are involved.

2. App Permissions & Usage Rationale

  • Storage Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to read PDF and image files you select for processing, and to save the output files to your device. Files are never copied to external locations or servers.
  • Granular Media Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES — Android 13+): On Android 13 and above, this permission is used instead of legacy storage permissions to access images you select for conversion or compression.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Declared for optional app update checks only. No user data, files, or analytics are transmitted over the network.

3. No Third-Party SDKs or Advertising

PDF HUB does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs. There are no ad networks, no Firebase, no Google Analytics — nothing that phones home. The app is entirely self-contained.

4. Local-Only File Processing

All operations — PDF merge, split, compress, image-to-PDF, PDF-to-image, image compress, resize, crop, and format conversion — are performed using on-device libraries (iText 7, Android Bitmap APIs). Your files are processed in local memory and saved directly to your device storage. No temporary copies are uploaded anywhere.

5. Data Security

Since no data leaves your device, there is no risk of server-side data breaches. Your files remain under your complete control at all times. You can delete any output files directly from your device's file manager or from PDF HUB's built-in File Manager.

6. Children's Privacy

PDF HUB does not collect any data from any user, including children. The app is safe for users of all ages as it performs no data collection whatsoever.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy (for example, if future versions add optional cloud features), the updated policy will be published at this URL and the "Last Updated" date will be revised. We will never retroactively collect data without your explicit consent.
