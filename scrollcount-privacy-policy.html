Official Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

At ScrollCount - Reels & Shorts (Application ID: com.techhub.scrollcount, developed by Krishna Tech Hub), your privacy is our core principle. ScrollCount is engineered as a 100% offline-first application designed to help users understand their short-form video consumption habits without compromising personal data.

🔒 Absolute Privacy Guarantee: ScrollCount does not request the android.permission.INTERNET permission. The app has zero network connectivity and cannot transmit data outside your physical device under any circumstance.

1. Prominent Disclosure: Android AccessibilityService Usage

ScrollCount requires the Android AccessibilityService API (android.permission.BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE) exclusively for its core functionality: detecting when Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts are actively playing on your screen to track screen time accurately.

  • Purpose of Use: The service inspects window state changes and UI node hierarchies solely to identify short-video player containers (e.g., Instagram Reels container or YouTube Shorts player) and measure viewing duration.
  • Strict Privacy Boundary: The Accessibility Service NEVER reads, accesses, logs, or monitors personal messages, chats, usernames, passwords, payment details, keystrokes, personal media, or search history.
  • No Background Audio or Video Capture: The service does not record audio, video, or screen pixels.
  • User Control: You can enable or disable this Accessibility Service at any time via your device's Android Settings > Accessibility.

2. Zero Data Collection & Zero Transmission

ScrollCount does not collect, harvest, sell, or share any personal information, location data, device identifiers, or usage logs. All aggregated statistics (daily scroll count, qualified view time) are stored strictly inside your device's encrypted private application storage.

3. No Third-Party SDKs or Advertising

ScrollCount is completely free from third-party tracking, analytics, and advertising libraries. There is no Firebase, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, Crashlytics, or AdMob integrated into the app. The codebase is fully self-contained.

4. Permissions Requested

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Used solely to display optional usage limit alerts and daily habit summaries directly on your device.
  • BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE: Used exclusively to detect active Reels/Shorts viewing sessions as described above.
  • INTERNET: NOT REQUESTED. The app contains no networking capabilities.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all usage data is stored purely on your local device, uninstalling the application or clearing the app data via Android Settings permanently and immediately erases all stored records.

6. Children's Privacy

ScrollCount does not collect data from any user, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe for all audiences.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be published immediately at this public URL with an updated effective date.
